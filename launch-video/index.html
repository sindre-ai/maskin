step 1 · feedback → bet
Feedback becomes an insight — then a bet.
Agents pull quantitative & qualitative feedback from your tools and cluster it into one clear insight.
step 2 · the strategist
A strategist agent proposes the bet.
It researches the problem and writes a hypothesis with a measurable outcome — you just steer.
step 3 · build
Coding & design agents build it.
The bet is decomposed into work and shipped to production — humans and agents in the same workspace.
step 4 · verify
Outcomes verified — the loop closes.
Post-launch, agents review fresh feedback and mark the bet succeeded or failed. Did we build the right thing?
