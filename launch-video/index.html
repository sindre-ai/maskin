step 1 · signal in
Feedback becomes an insight — then a bet.
Agents pull quantitative & qualitative signals from Slack, Intercom & PostHog and cluster them automatically.
step 2 · you decide
Humans approve the bet. Agents do the rest.
A strategist agent writes the hypothesis and outcome. You steer — judgment is the part that doesn’t delegate.
step 3 · work done
Agents build it — in your existing tools.
Design & coding agents ship through Slack, GitHub & Linear. No new stack, no glue work.
step 4 · loop closed
Outcome measured. Customer notified.
Post-launch, agents verify the result and mark the bet succeeded or failed. The organisation learns.
