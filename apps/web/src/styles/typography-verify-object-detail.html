Long-form prose (75ch cap at ≥1280px)

We believe replacing the current Inter+v0 typography pass with the maskin.io family stack and shipping a documented weight ramp plus a measure cap on the three primary reading surfaces will produce app typography that feels visually continuous with the marketing surface and reads like a well-crafted Linear issue at any viewport.

Below 1280px the prose fills the available width inside the max-w-3xl column that the detail page already uses. At 1280px and above, it caps at 75ch so the reader's eye does not have to jump across a wide line — the single most defensible UX claim on the bet, per T2's design proposal. Resize the window past the 1280px breakpoint to see the cap engage.
