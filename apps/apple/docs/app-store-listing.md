## Description (≤4000)
Maskin is a shared workspace where your team and its AI agents work together. People set direction; agents do the work and ask when they need a decision. This app keeps you in the loop wherever you are.

• For You: the decisions that need you, one at a time. Approve, reject or reply.
• Chats: talk with teammates and agents in shared conversations. Attach photos and files, dictate a message and mention people.
• Bets, tasks and insights: browse and update the work your team is running.
• Agents and loops: see what your agents are doing and how automated work is progressing.
• Share to Maskin: send a link, text, image or PDF from any app straight into your workspace.
• Notifications and widgets: know when something needs you.

Sign in with your existing Maskin account. You can create one at maskin.io.
